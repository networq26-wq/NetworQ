Delete your NetworQ account

For the NetworQ app on Android and the web

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open NetworQ and sign in.
  2. Tap your profile, then Settings.
  3. Scroll to Delete account, type DELETE and confirm.

You'll get an email confirming the date. Your account is deleted permanently after 7 days. Signing in before then lets you cancel.

Without the app

Email privacy@networq.co.in from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my NetworQ account". We'll confirm within 7 days.

What is deleted

Your profile and photo, contacts, notes, reminders, scanned cards, follow-up history, meetings, Event Radar memberships and connection requests, sign-in device records, and your login.

What may be kept

Contacts that other users saved after you accepted their connection request stay in their accounts. Server logs are removed within 30 days.
