Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

NetworQ helps professionals save contacts, follow up and meet people at events. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Event Radar and Bluetooth

When Radar is on, your phone broadcasts a random identifier that changes every 15 minutes. It contains no personal data. Radar only starts after you agree to it on your phone. Only people in the same event, or on Nearby, with Radar on, can see your public profile (name, title, company, photo) and an approximate distance range. Your contact details are shared only when you accept a connection request. We never collect your location. On Android 11 and below, the system calls the Bluetooth permission "Location"; NetworQ does not use it to determine where you are.

We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Who can see your information

Your contacts, notes, scans, reminders and drafts are private to your account. People you connect or chat with see your profile (name, role, company, photo and the details you choose to share on your card) and the messages you send them. On Radar, people nearby see only what your Radar privacy settings allow. When you send a follow-up email, the recipient sees your name, your reply-to email address and the message.

How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account is open. When you delete your account, there is a 7-day grace period in which you can cancel; after that we delete your profile, contacts, scans, events, chats you're part of, connections and settings. Copies in our database provider's backups are overwritten within 30 days. We keep the following for longer only where needed: records of reports and actions taken on abuse (up to 1 year, for safety); and email delivery logs held by our email provider for their standard retention period. Voice recordings are not stored after they are turned into text.

Your choices and rights

  • Edit your profile and notification preferences in Settings.
  • Export your contacts as CSV at any time.
  • Turn Radar off or go incognito per event.
  • Delete your account in Settings → Delete account or see how to delete your account. Deletion completes after a 7-day grace period and removes your profile, contacts, scans, events and connection data.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Row-level security ensures each user can only access their own records. You can sign out of all devices from Settings.

Children

NetworQ is for professionals aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18; if you believe a child has used NetworQ, contact us and we will delete the account.
