Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

NetworQ helps professionals save contacts, follow up and meet people at events. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

DataWhy
Account: email, password (stored hashed by our auth provider), name, company, role, phone, LinkedIn, profile photoTo run your account and show your profile to people you choose to connect with
Contacts you add or scan, notes, reminders, follow-up draftsCore features — they are private to your account
Business-card photos you scanTo extract contact details with AI; stored in your private folder
Event Radar: events you join, Radar settings, connection requestsTo let attendees of the same event discover each other
Sign-in devices (browser/app name and a one-way fingerprint)To alert you about sign-ins from new devices

Event Radar and Bluetooth

When Radar is on, your phone broadcasts a random identifier that changes every 15 minutes. It contains no personal data. Only attendees of the same event, with Radar on, can see your public profile (name, title, company, photo) and an approximate distance range. Your contact details are shared only when you accept a connection request. We never collect your location. On Android 11 and below, the system calls the Bluetooth permission "Location"; NetworQ does not use it to determine where you are.

Service providers

SupabaseDatabase, authentication and file storage
GroqAI processing of card images and assistant messages (not used to train models under their API terms)
ResendSending emails you request (follow-ups, invites) and account emails
Google (optional)Sign in with Google and Calendar invites, only if you choose them

We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Your choices and rights

  • Edit your profile and notification preferences in Settings.
  • Export your contacts as CSV at any time.
  • Turn Radar off or go incognito per event.
  • Delete your account in Settings → Delete account or see how to delete your account. Deletion completes after a 7-day grace period and removes your profile, contacts, scans, events and connection data.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Row-level security ensures each user can only access their own records. You can sign out of all devices from Settings.

Children

NetworQ is for professionals aged 18 and over.

Contact

Questions or requests: privacy@networq.co.in
