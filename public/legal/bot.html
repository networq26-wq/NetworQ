NetworQBot

User agent: Mozilla/5.0 (compatible; NetworQBot/1.0; +https://www.networq.co.in/bot)

NetworQBot helps professionals discover upcoming public business and tech events. It reads only the public schema.org Event data that organisers publish (name, date, venue, link), and every event in NetworQ links back to its original page.

How it behaves

  • Follows robots.txt, including rules for NetworQBot.
  • Visits a small number of listing pages per site every 6 hours and waits between requests.
  • Does not log in, submit forms, or collect personal data about attendees.

Opt out

Add User-agent: NetworQBot / Disallow: / to your robots.txt, or email support@networq.co.in and we'll stop within 24 hours.
