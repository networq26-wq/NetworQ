# NetworQ — client pitch, point by point

*Every claim below is a working feature you can show live. Nothing here is a roadmap item unless it says "Next".*

---

## 1. The hook (15 seconds)
> "How many people did you meet at the last event — and how many did you actually follow up with?"

Most people meet 20 and follow up with 2. The other 18 were opportunities: clients, partners, investors, hires. **NetworQ makes sure you never lose a conversation again.**

## 2. The problem (30 seconds)
- Cards go in a drawer. Notes live in five apps. LinkedIn shows *who* you know, not *what you talked about* or *what you promised*.
- CRMs are built for sales ops, not for the person in a conference hallway with a coffee in one hand.
- Follow-ups depend on memory — and memory loses.

## 3. What NetworQ does — one sentence each
1. **Capture in seconds** — scan a card, or just *say* who you met; the AI writes it down and saves the contact with a reminder.
2. **Know who's next** — the home screen tells you exactly who's waiting to hear from you, and why.
3. **Follow up in one tap** — personal AI emails for one person or fifty; meeting invites that land in their calendar.
4. **Stay connected** — private messages and voice/video calls with your connections, inside the app.
5. **Meet the right people** — Radar finds NetworQ people around you (Bluetooth, no location), and Events shows real events near you.
6. **Share yourself beautifully** — a premium digital card any phone camera can save.

## 4. Why it's different
| Others (Blinq, Popl, HiHello, Linq…) | NetworQ |
|---|---|
| Stop at exchanging a card | Goes all the way: **discover → capture → remember → follow up → talk → meet** |
| Often need NFC hardware | **No hardware** — camera, voice, QR and Bluetooth on the phone you have |
| Generic AI text, or none | **Fact-checked AI research**: reads the company's public site and cites sources; never invents |
| Contact list | **Who's next** — prioritised follow-ups with reasons |
| Separate apps to message/call | **Messages + voice & video calls** built in, only between real connections |
| Location-based "people near you" | **Privacy-first Radar**: rotating anonymous IDs, no location collected, block anytime |

## 5. Who it's for (pick the slide for the room)
**Founders & BD teams** — every event becomes a pipeline: scan the stack of cards at night, send personal follow-ups in the morning, book meetings that land in calendars.

**Sales teams** — voice-note a lead in the hallway; AI fills the details; reminders make sure nobody goes cold. Select a segment → email them all, personally.

**Investors** — remember every founder, the context, and the promised next step; research their company in one tap.

**Event organisers (B2B)** — give attendees an **Event Radar code**: they see who's at the event, connect, and chat in the event room. Your event becomes the place people actually meet. *(Ask: "What if your attendees left with 10 real connections each?")*

## 6. Proof it works (say it, then show it)
- Live at **networq.co.in** on web, with an **Android app** (iOS next).
- Real data only: real events from Luma, Eventbrite and Meetup; no sample content.
- Engineering quality: 150+ automated end-to-end tests on phone and desktop plus database security tests run before every release; access rules enforced in the database (only connected, unblocked people can message or call).
- Privacy: Bluetooth IDs rotate every 15 minutes, no location stored, calls are peer-to-peer and encrypted.

## Objections — short answers
| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "We already use LinkedIn." | LinkedIn is who you know. NetworQ is what you talked about, what you promised, and the follow-up actually happening. |
| "We have a CRM." | NetworQ is where the conversation is captured — in the hallway, in 10 seconds. CRM sync is on the roadmap. |
| "Is it safe?" | Access rules are enforced in the database; Radar uses rotating anonymous IDs and no location; calls are encrypted peer-to-peer; you can block anyone and delete your account. |
| "Does it work in India?" | Built India-first: Indian events, Whisper speech that understands Indian English and Hindi, works on any Android phone. |
| "What if the AI gets it wrong?" | It never invents: it leaves blanks rather than guessing, and you review before anything is saved or sent. |
| "iPhone?" | Works in Safari today; the native iOS app is next. |
