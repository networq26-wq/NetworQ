# Live demo — 7 minutes

**Setup (5 min before):** two phones (or phone + laptop) signed in as two connected accounts ("You" and "Colleague"); a real business card; Wi-Fi on; phone volume up; notifications allowed; dark mode off (projects better). Close other apps.

| Min | Show | Say | Tap path |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | **Home** | "This is my day. NetworQ tells me who's waiting to hear from me — and why." | People tab → point at the greeting line, *Who's next*, *Coming up* |
| 0:45 | **Scan a card** | "I met someone. Ten seconds." | **Scan** → photograph the card → details appear → **Save** |
| 1:30 | **Voice note** | "No card? I just talk." | **Voice** → tap mic → *"Met Priya, head of growth at Zoho, wants a demo next week, remind me Thursday"* → tap stop → **Continue** → show filled details + "Thu" reminder → **Save contact** |
| 2:30 | **Who's next → follow-up** | "Now the part everyone forgets." | Back on People → Priya is in *Who's next* → **Email** → show the personal draft → **Send** |
| 3:15 | **Select & bulk** | "After an event I follow up with everyone at once — each email is personal." | **Select** → tick 3 → **Email 3** → show the drafts → (don't send) |
| 3:45 | **Meeting** | "Book the meeting — it lands in their calendar." | Contact → **Meet** → 30 min, Google Meet or Jitsi → **Send** → show success |
| 4:30 | **Messages + call** | "And I can talk to my connections right here." | **Messages** → **New message** → Colleague → send "Hi!" → second phone shows it → tap **Video** → second phone rings → **Accept** → wave → **End** |
| 5:45 | **Events + Radar** | "Where do I meet more people? Real events near me — and at the event, Radar shows who's there." | **Events** → location → *Hyderabad* → card → **Going** → Event Radar opens |
| 6:30 | **My card** | "And sharing myself is one tap." | Avatar → card flips to QR → another phone's camera scans → contact saved |
| 7:00 | **Close** | "Never lose a conversation again. Want to try it at your next event?" | — |

**If something fails:** say "network's slow here — let me show you on the other phone", or skip to the next step. Never blame the user's question.

**Don't:** demo Bluetooth Radar on stage unless both phones are tested in that room; quote prices; promise roadmap dates.
