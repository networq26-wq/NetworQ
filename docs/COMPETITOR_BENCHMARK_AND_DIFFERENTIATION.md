## 1. Executive Summary

Most existing products in this category operate primarily as **Digital Business Cards** (NFC accessories, static landing pages, or basic vCard generators). Their product journey ends at the exchange of contact details:

```text
MEET → TAP / SCAN → SAVE TO PHONE CONTACTS → [FLOW STOPS]
```

**NetworQ** is architected as an end-to-end **Professional Discovery, Relationship Intelligence & AI-Powered Networking CRM**:

```text
DISCOVER (Radar/BLE) → MEET → CONNECT (Mutual Request/Accept) → UNDERSTAND (AI Intelligence) → FOLLOW UP (A/B/C Sourced Emails) → BUILD RELATIONSHIP (CRM Timeline & Intros)
```

## 3. NetworQ's 10 Core Product Differentiators (Part 62)

1. **Professional Proximity Radar:**
   - Real Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) peripheral advertising and scanning.
   - Cryptographically rotating 15-minute ephemeral tokens.
   - Privacy-first: users are only visible when the Radar screen is active; blocks take effect within 15 seconds across mutual devices.

2. **Event Radar & Attendee Relevance:**
   - Event-specific discovery filtering by attendee intents ("Looking for: Clients, Partners, Investors, Collaborators").
   - Transparent, non-sensitive matching rationale explaining shared context without creepy profiling.

3. **AI Relationship Intelligence:**
   - Real-time synthesis: `Who → Company → Context → Opportunity → Next Action`.
   - Autonomous background website and business profile research.

4. **Batch Scan-to-CRM with Strict Data Segregation:**
   - Direct camera launch without intermediate friction.
   - Multi-card batch processing (up to 25 cards) with live progress tracking (`Processing X / Y`).
   - Clean segregation: Person, Company, Social, Address, and Other.
   - "Needs Review" highlighting for ambiguous OCR fields; never guesses or hallucinates.

5. **Integrated Relationship Timeline:**
   - Complete history per relationship: `Connected → Event Met → Voice Notes → Sent Emails → Meetings → Next Follow-up`.

6. **Fact-Checked AI Email Engine (A/B/C Options):**
   - Option A: Human/Contextual (driven by user notes).
   - Option B: Research-Driven (backed by verified quotes from official company website).
   - Option C: Value-Focused (tailored to our organization's specific service offerings).
   - Strict factual validation: unverified claims are filtered out before reaching the user; requires mandatory two-tap confirmation before sending.

7. **Mutual Introductions Engine:**
   - Request an introduction to any 2nd-degree target through a verified mutual connection.
   - Structured 3-way notification and acceptance loop.

8. **Networking Day Summary & Action Plan:**
   - Automatically computes people met, unique companies, and priority follow-ups post-event.
   - Generates an actionable executive follow-up plan with one click.

9. **Professional Network Map:**
   - Interactive visual graph displaying `Me → Direct Connections → 2nd-Degree Touchpoints → Company Clusters`.
   - Instant search and introduction pathways.

10. **Contextual 3D Digital Pass & Offline Resilience:**
    - Four purpose-built modes: **Normal** (Member identity), **Event** (Attendee goals), **Sales** (Services pitch + booking URL), **Speaker** (Keynote session topic).
    - Standard vCard 3.0 QR code with embedded contextual notes readable by any smartphone camera with zero app install required.
    - Full offline capability: pass, QR, and cached contacts function with zero internet connectivity.
